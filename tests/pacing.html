Vsync pacing: same GPU workload, different admission policies

This runs three continuous phases in the same mounted surface: paced → unbounded → paced (or reversed). Fonts, pixels, resolution, data and shaders are unchanged between arms. No per-frame fence waits or GPU readback occurs during a phase. Measurements distinguish rAF callbacks, actual submissions and capacity deferrals. Neither callback cadence nor queue notifications measure physical scanout or compositor dropped frames.
